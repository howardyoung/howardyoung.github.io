About

Howard O Young is an American inventor, entrepreneur, and technology executive based in Dothan, Alabama. He is the Founder and Managing Director of Howard Young Tech, established in 2023. With over 40 years of experience spanning embedded systems, storage solutions, cloud infrastructure, and enterprise technology, Young has held senior engineering and executive roles across leading aerospace and enterprise storage organizations.

Young earned a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics and Computer Science from California Lutheran University in Thousand Oaks, California, establishing the mathematical and analytical foundation for a career in compiler design, low-level firmware, Fibre Channel architectures, and cloud hydrations.

Of American Indian (Potawatomi tribe) and Norwegian descent, Young grew up working in his family's store, The Buffalo Robe, in Reseda, California. His grandfather was an inventor and geologist who operated oil wells in Baldwin Hills during the 1920s, including the derrick Phil Young, Jr., establishing a multigenerational legacy of engineering and enterprise.
